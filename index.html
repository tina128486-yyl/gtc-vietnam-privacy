# Privacy Policy **GTC Social Media Publisher** **Effective date:** October 17, 2026 ## 1. Introduction GTC Social Media Publisher (“the App”) is an internal business tool intended to help authorized users manage and publish social media content for business purposes. This Privacy Policy explains how the App may access, use, and handle information when an authorized user connects a LinkedIn account or LinkedIn Page. ## 2. Information We Access Depending on the permissions granted by LinkedIn and the features used, the App may access: - Basic LinkedIn profile information needed to authorize the connection. - LinkedIn organization or Page information needed to identify the authorized Page. - Content that an authorized user chooses to publish through the App. - Authorization tokens or related technical information required to maintain the connection. The App does not intentionally request LinkedIn account passwords. ## 3. How We Use Information Information accessed through the App is used only for authorized business functions, such as: - Connecting an authorized LinkedIn account. - Identifying the LinkedIn Page selected by an authorized user. - Preparing and publishing content explicitly submitted by an authorized user. - Maintaining, troubleshooting, and securing the App. We do not sell personal information. ## 4. Data Storage and Security Access to LinkedIn information is limited to what is necessary for the App's intended functions and the permissions granted by LinkedIn. Technical information and authorization credentials may be processed or stored by the systems used to operate the App. Appropriate safeguards should be applied to protect this information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss. Information will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purposes or as required by applicable law. ## 5. Sharing of Information We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the App, or disclosed when required by law. Content submitted for publication will be sent to LinkedIn in accordance with the user's authorization and LinkedIn's applicable terms. ## 6. LinkedIn Authorization and Revocation Users can control the permissions they grant through LinkedIn. They may revoke the App's access through the relevant LinkedIn account settings. Revoking access may prevent some or all App functions from working. Revocation does not necessarily delete information already published on LinkedIn. Published content can generally be managed through LinkedIn. ## 7. Data Deletion Requests To ask questions about personal information processed by the App or request deletion of information held by the App operator, contact us using the contact details below. We will review requests and handle them in accordance with applicable law and any retention obligations. Information that remains on LinkedIn or is held by another service may need to be deleted through that service separately. ## 8. Children's Privacy The App is intended for business users and is not designed for children. ## 9. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL with an updated effective date where appropriate. ## 10.
