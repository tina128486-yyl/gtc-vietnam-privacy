Privacy Policy

Personal Social Media Publishing Tool

Effective date: October 19, 2026

1. Introduction

This privacy policy describes how the operator of a personal social media publishing tool may process information when the operator connects social media accounts and uses the tool to prepare or publish content. Supported platforms may include X, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, and other services, depending on the integrations configured.

The tool is intended for personal account management. It is not an official product of, or endorsed by, X, Meta, Google, or other social media platforms.

2. Information That May Be Processed

Depending on the platform, the permissions granted, and the features used, the tool may process:

  • Basic account or profile information made available through an authorized connection.
  • Account, channel, or page identifiers needed to select the intended destination.
  • Text, images, videos, and other content that the user chooses to prepare or publish.
  • Authorization tokens or related technical information required for an integration to function.
  • Technical logs or diagnostic information, if generated by the tool or its supporting services.

The information actually accessed depends on the permissions approved by the user and the capabilities of each platform integration. The tool is not intended to collect social media passwords.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information may be processed to:

  • Connect and manage social media accounts authorized by the user.
  • Identify the account, channel, or page selected for publishing.
  • Prepare, schedule, or publish content when the user requests these actions and the integration supports them.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and protect the tool and its integrations.

Content is submitted to a social media platform only through the relevant integration and subject to the user's actions, granted permissions, and the platform's rules.

4. Storage and Security

Information may be processed by the device or environment running the tool, by services used to operate it, and by the connected social media platforms. The actual storage location and handling of authorization tokens, logs, and other information depend on the tool's configuration and the services in use.

The operator should verify the tool's actual data flows and apply appropriate safeguards to information under the operator's control. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Sharing and Third-Party Services

Information may be transmitted to a social media platform when the user authorizes an account connection or requests an action such as publishing content. Technical information may also be handled by service providers required for the tool to operate, depending on the configuration.

Each connected platform processes information under its own privacy policy and terms. The operator does not control the independent privacy practices of those platforms or other third-party services.

6. Permissions and Revoking Access

Users can manage the permissions granted to connected platforms and, where supported, revoke an application's access through the relevant platform settings. Revoking access may disable some features.

Revoking access does not necessarily delete information already transmitted to a platform or content already published there. Published content and platform-held data may need to be managed separately through the relevant platform.

7. Data Retention and Deletion Requests

Information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it is processed, subject to applicable legal obligations. The specific retention period depends on the actual configuration and the services involved.

For questions about information handled by the tool operator, or to request deletion of information under the operator's control, contact the email address listed below. Please do not include passwords, authorization tokens, or other secret credentials in a request.

Information held by a social media platform or another service may need to be deleted through that service separately.

8. Children's Privacy

This tool is intended for personal social media management and is not designed for children. Users should comply with the age requirements and terms of the platforms they use.
